Hologram Hot Stamping Foil

High-security holographic foil applied using heat and pressure to create a permanent anti-counterfeit mark on packaging and labels.

Custom security hologram solution

We design and supply hologram security solutions ranging from high-security tamper-evident labels to scalable authentication formats for everyday packaging. Each format can be aligned to your brand identity, verification workflow, and supply chain environment.

Benefits of Hologram Hot Stamping Foil

  • Creates a durable authentication feature that is difficult to duplicate.
  • Improves shelf appeal with premium metallic holographic impact.
  • Works across carton packs, mono cartons, and branded label zones.

Typical applications

  • FMCG carton branding
  • Pharmaceutical outer packs
  • Premium product seals
