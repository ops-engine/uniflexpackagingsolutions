3 Side Seal Pouch

A 3-side seal pouch is a type of flexible packaging with three sealed edges and one open edge used for filling. The open edge can be sealed through heat sealing or zipper conversion based on product needs.

It is also known as a flat pouch and is widely used for single serves, sample packs, powders, and snack products where economical packing and clean presentation are important.

Common structure and material choices include:

  • Plastic structures such as PE, PP, and multilayer laminates
  • Paper-based options for recyclable-forward applications
  • Metalized and foil structures for higher oxygen and moisture barrier
+ - Benefits of 3 side seal pouch ›
  • Flexible packaging that is inexpensive
  • Larger bag capacity than many four-side-sealed variants
  • Easy product access with tear notch options
  • Good package consistency for cartoning
  • High-speed packaging applications are possible
  • Affordable transportation and storage
