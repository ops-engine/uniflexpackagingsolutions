Retort Pouch

A retort pouch is a flexible laminate package designed to withstand high-temperature thermal processing under pressure, offering an efficient alternative to conventional canning.

This processing method supports sterile packaging performance and stable shelf life for ready meals, sauces, and other thermally processed foods.

Common structure and material choices include:

  • Polyester (PET) outer layers for heat and mechanical resistance
  • Aluminium barrier layers for oxygen and moisture protection
  • CPP sealant layers for retort-compatible sealing performance
+ - Benefits of retort pouch ›
  • Food flavor retention after processing
  • Constructed from multiple premium layers
  • Can be supplied in resealable and convenience variants
  • Lower pack weight than many rigid options
  • Strong shelf stability
  • Good portability for prepared foods
+ - Applications (industries) ›
  • Prepared and ready-to-eat meals (MREs)
  • Field meals
  • Condiments
  • Camping, boating, hiking, and trekking rations
  • Pet food formats
  • Thermally processed packaged foods
