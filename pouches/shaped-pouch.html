Shaped Pouch

Shaped pouches are manufactured in custom forms rather than standard rectangular formats, helping brands create distinctive shelf differentiation.

They can be developed with bespoke contours, rounded options, and functional add-ons such as spouts for easier pouring and handling.

Common structure and material choices include:

  • Polyester (PET) for print quality and structural strength
  • Nylon layers for puncture resistance
  • Aluminium and sealant layers for barrier and sealing performance
+ - Benefits of shaped pouch ›
  • Unique appearance
  • Many colors for printing
  • Different pouch shape possibilities
  • Food safety material combinations
  • High puncture resistance options
  • Premium shelf differentiation
+ - Applications (industries) ›
  • Dry food and powders
  • Soups, sauces and spices
  • Snack foods
  • Confectionery
  • Pet food and treats
  • Specialty consumer products
