Flat Bottom Pouch

A flat bottom pouch is a flexible packaging format with a stable flat base that stands upright and supports premium shelf presentation.

Compared with rounded-bottom variants, flat bottom structures improve shelf organization and offer multiple branding panels with higher pack volume efficiency.

Common structure and material choices include:

  • Polyethylene (PE) for moisture resistance and durability
  • Polypropylene (PP) for robust handling and pack strength
  • Barrier laminate structures for aroma and shelf-life performance
+ - Benefits of flat bottom pouch ›
  • Efficient utilization of shelf space
  • Up to 4 panels for brand messaging
  • Full-height gussets for higher volume
  • Strong standing ability on shelf
  • Improved retail display presence
  • Premium pack appearance
+ - Applications (industries) ›
  • Coffee
  • Snack foods
  • Pet food
  • Frozen foods
  • Nutritional products
  • Specialty dry products
