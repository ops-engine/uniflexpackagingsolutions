4 Side Seal Pouch

A 4-side seal pouch provides better leak control and product protection than many 3-side formats because each edge is completely sealed.

This format is widely used where pack integrity is critical and can be produced in multiple sizes and structures based on product and filling conditions.

Common structure and material choices include:

  • Plastic laminates such as PE, PP, and barrier combinations
  • Paper-based structures for food and recyclable-forward usage
  • Metalized and foil layers for moisture and oxygen protection
+ - Benefits of 4 side seal pouch ›
  • Flexible packaging that is inexpensive
  • Good package security against leaks
  • Easy access with tear-notch features
  • High-speed packaging applications are possible
  • Affordable transportation and storage
  • Suitable for sensitive product categories
+ - Applications (industries) ›
  • Liquids
  • Medical
  • Nutritional products
  • Household chemicals
  • Meal kits
  • Coffee, snack foods, frozen foods, and pet food
